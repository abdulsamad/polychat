# PolyChat launch video

- **Angle:** one workspace for every AI model, and a blind vote to make them prove they're the best.
- **Tone:** punchy, playful, clean. Dark theme, real tokens, Inter and Fira Code.
- **Music:** 120 BPM in A minor, every cut on a beat.
- **Stand-ins:** prompts, answers, images and metrics are sample content, labelled "Sample answers and data" in the video.
